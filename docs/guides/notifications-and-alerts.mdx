## Overview

Tako can dispatch real-time alerts whenever critical cluster events occur, including:
- Deployment successes and failures.
- Node disconnects or degraded health status.
- Container health check probe failures.
- Backup completion or failure notices.

## Setting Up Notifications

1. In the Tako Console, go to **Settings** > **Notifications**.
2. Select your channel provider (Discord, Slack, or Generic Webhook).
3. Paste your Webhook URL.
4. Select the events you want to be notified about:
   - `[x]` Deployments (Success / Failed)
   - `[x]` Node Outages & Latency Spikes
   - `[x]` Container Crash Loops
   - `[x]` Scheduled Backup Status

### Testing Your Channel

Click the **Test Channel** button to trigger `/api/v1/settings/notifications/test`. Tako will dispatch a sample event payload to your webhook. Check your Discord or Slack channel to verify that the message arrives!
